Round three

Thank you for the second round of answers and photographs — everything you asked for is in. Here is what changed, and the few things still worth a word.

Still nothing here is public. Your real website is untouched and this draft is still hidden from Google. Only people you give the link to can find it.

What changed

  • The home page now says the beanbags help children and adults, made one at a time by Julie Hannon and her team — and your guarantee paragraph is on it: backed by a 12-year guarantee on both the outer cover and the inner lining, built from the earliest playdays to the teenage years. You said 12 or 15, whichever sounds believable — 12 went on because it is the figure in your own paragraph. One word to make it 15.
  • The About page now says you spent years watching how children use movement and play to explore their physical capabilities and make sense of the world around them.
  • Prices: XXX-Large is now €415, and lycra is €15 more than the fleece price — so Large €240 and X-Large €275. Glance at those two figures and say if they are not what you meant.
  • Courier now reads “€10–15 depending on weight” everywhere it comes up.
  • The fleece-versus-lycra table says medium stretch, a gentle gradual sink-in (and flops!) for fleece, an instant deep sink-in for lycra.
  • Washing: the fleece instructions now open with “the cover is easily removed for washing” and “a Medium beanbag can be washed and tumble dried as one unit — the beads stay in the bag”; “wash the lycra on its own” is gone; and “soaking overnight” is in the weighted-items care list.
  • Forest Green is in the colour range, using your photo.
  • Fabric swatches: “Can’t decide on a colour? Try before you buy” is under the colours — up to five swatches for €5. You wrote $5; it went on as euro.
  • A new page for schools and professionals in Cork — the two-week trial, how it came about, calm-focus-and-regulation, your “I’d love you to try it” invitation, Request a quote and Request a visit buttons, ETB registered, and the discount for buying more than one product. It is in the menu as “For schools”, and the home and ordering pages point to it.
  • The school’s reference is on that page — signed “ASD class teacher, secondary school, Co. Cork” with no name, as you asked, until you make contact with the school. It was also lightly trimmed where it said “Sensory Products” rather than Sensory Beanbags; say if you would rather have it word for word.
  • Your photographs: the pink and turquoise lap pads with the navy snake replaced the lap pad picture on the weighted products page, with your measurements as the caption — if you meant the group photo on the home page instead, say so and it moves. The classroom with the X-Large lycra beanbags is on the new schools page.
  • The rainbow blanket photo stayed. You said keep it if it is better, and it is — the new one on the grass has a hard shadow and the cardboard shows. The others you sent (the navy snake, the pile on the green beanbag, the pink lap pad close-up) are kept aside rather than on, so the pages stay easy to read. Any of them can go in with a word.
  • Payment: ETB registered is on the ordering page and the schools page, alongside Revolut, bank transfer and cash on delivery in Cork. Stripe is left as it is for the moment, as you said.
