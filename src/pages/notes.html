For Julie

A few questions

This is a draft of a possible new website for Sensory Beanbags. Have a look around it, tell me what you think, and if you have a minute, answer whatever you can below.

Nothing here is public. Your real website is untouched and still running exactly as it was. This draft is deliberately hidden from Google, so nobody will stumble across it and no customer will see it by accident. Only people you give the link to can find it.

Everything on it — the prices, sizes, colours, photographs, the words — came from your own website, so it should mostly look familiar. Where I could not work something out, I have asked about it below rather than guess.

You do not need to answer all of this. Answer what is easy, ignore the rest, and just tell me anything that is wrong. A phone call is honestly the quickest way — my number is at the bottom.

1. Photographs — the most useful thing you could send

This is where the draft is weakest, and it is the easiest thing to fix.

  1. A photo of a lycra beanbag

    I could not find one anywhere. Every beanbag photo on your website is fleece, so on the lycra section there is an empty grey box where a picture should be. Since the whole point of lycra is that it looks and feels different from fleece, showing a fleece one instead would be misleading.

    A photo taken on your phone would do the job. This is the only product on the site with nothing to show for it, and it is the one you describe as new.

  2. A photo of you

    There is no photograph of you anywhere on your current site. On the “About Julie” page there is another empty box waiting for one. You at the sewing machine, or in the workshop with a finished beanbag, would be perfect. People buying something hand-made like to see who made it.

  3. Any newer photos generally

    The pictures I used are yours, but most of them are from between 2011 and 2015. Anything more recent, taken on a modern phone, would lift the whole site. No need for a photographer.

2. Two things your website says twice, differently

The XXX-Large price

Your shop says €425. Your price list page says €415. I have used €425 for now. Which is right?

The colour range

Your price list and your shop offer different colours. The price list mentions Mid Grey, Emerald Green and Orange; the shop instead offers Mid Blue and Pale Lavender. I have shown the price list version. Which is current?

3. Do you still make these?

I found photographs of products that are not for sale on your site any more. If you still make them, they should be on here.

  • Mini beanbags and small hand beanbags — small textured ones.
  • A Small beanbag size, below the Medium your price list starts at.

If they are discontinued, no problem — I will leave them off.

4. The online shop

This is the biggest open question, and only you can answer it.

Your current website has a shopping cart where people can pay by PayPal. This draft does not — it asks people to ring or email you instead. That was my assumption, not a decision, and it is easily changed.

So:

  • Does anyone actually buy through the cart? Roughly how many orders a year come through the website, compared to the phone and email?
  • Would you miss it? Everything you make is to order, in a chosen size, weight and colour — which is really a conversation. If you are having that conversation on the phone anyway, the cart may not be doing much.
  • If you want one, that is completely fine. It can be added back, and card payments would be handled by a company that specialises in it, so card details never come near the website.

5. Three things that are not written down anywhere

Your current site does not mention any of these, and customers do ask.

  1. Returns and cancellations

    What happens if somebody changes their mind? Because everything is made to order, you have more protection than an ordinary shop — but it still has to be written down somewhere for customers to read.

  2. What “CE approved” covers

    Your site says this, and I have kept it. I found a signed certificate from 2017 among your files. What exactly does it cover, and should we show it on the site? It is a good thing to be able to prove.

  3. Delivery outside Ireland

    Do you send to Northern Ireland or Britain, and if so what does it cost? Nothing on your site mentions it either way.

Two smaller ones: are the washing instructions for the weighted products right (I guessed those), and do you have more customer testimonials? Only one survived on your current site, and they are worth a lot to someone deciding.

What I changed, and why

In case you are wondering where things went.

Your website had around fourteen pages, several covering the same ground. This draft has five, so nothing is buried. The shop, the individual product pages and the beanbag information page are now one page with a proper size and price table. The FAQ, ordering and delivery information are now one page.

I removed the four long articles about autism. They read as though they were written for search engines rather than for the people who buy from you, and the genuinely useful parts — how children actually use the beanbags, how to choose a weight — are now on the product pages where someone deciding will actually see them.

Your phone number and email are now on every page, and tapping the number on a phone rings you. The size guide shows dimensions and prices side by side. The whole thing works properly on a phone, and it works for visitors using a screen reader, which matters more than usual for your customers.

If you disagree with any of that, say so. It is your business and none of it is fixed.
