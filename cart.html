Before you pay

  • Made to order. Beanbags usually take five to seven working days, and are then sent by courier or An Post, or delivered by Julie in Cork city. Because each one is made in your choice of size and colour, it cannot be returned simply because you have changed your mind — see how to order. If there is a fault, Julie puts it right at her own cost.
  • Ireland only. PayPal asks for your delivery address during payment. If the address is outside Ireland the payment is not taken.
  • Buying more than one? Discounts are available — email Julie before paying and she will quote you.
  • Want it talked through first? Call 087 131 9619. Julie is happy to advise on size, colour and fabric before anything is made.
  • Your details. Payment is handled by PayPal, and your card details never reach this website. What Julie receives with your order, and how it is used, is on the privacy page.
