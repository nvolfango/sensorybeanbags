Description

56″ Wide

Suitable for a few children to relax in and wonderful for hugging together for  story time.  The X-Large XX-Large and XXX-Large  offers more of the sensory effect.  The size and   design leads to more ‘give’ in the beanbag so it is good for sprawling , jumping into, rolling about in.  When there are fewer beans in the beanbag, a child can be  wrapped up in one  like a hotdog.  For armchair effect and  full back support the beanbag can be turned on its side giving another kind of  steady comfort.
