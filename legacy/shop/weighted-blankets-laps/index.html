Description

All blankets are suitable as a throw blanket for home, school and travel.  For a single bed, Large size is suitable for children age 7 and under while the X-Large is more suitable for children over 7 years of age.  The XX-Large Blanket is wider and so more suitable for adults.

We generally have a number of medium and large blankets in stock (5lb to 8lb, Royal Blue) which are the ones most commonly ordered.  All other sizes, weights and colours take 2-3 weeks from time of order.

 (see full sizes here).

Research and experience has shown that the weight should be approximately 10% of a child’s body weight to be effective.  This is just a guide however, as each individual is different.  Some may like their blankets heavier and some may prefer them lighter. A weighted blanket however should never be used as a restraint,  and should never cover the client/child’s face.  For safety reasons, a weighted blanket should NEVER be used with an individual unless they can independently remove the blanket themselves when needed.

Care and washing of Weighted Blankets

The weighted pad/blanket can be machine washed and dried on a gentle cycle.  However, for preservation of fabric it is often recommended to hand wash and line dry.   Because this is a speciality item one thinks twice before washing and sometimes all that is needed is to wash a section of the blanket.
