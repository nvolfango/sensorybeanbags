Choosing the right weight

This is the question we are asked most. Here is how we normally think about it.

  • Lap pads: around 5% of the child's body weight is the usual starting point.
  • A three-year-old is typically well suited to a 1.5 lb or 2 lb lap pad.
  • Blankets for young children: 2 lb or 2.5 lb, at roughly 97 × 92 cm.
  • Lighter is a fine place to start. A small amount of weight is often enough to be reassuring and to help with self-soothing.

Please check with your occupational therapist before ordering. They know the child, and weight, size and how long a product is used for should all be matched to them. A weighted product should never be used on a child who cannot move it off themselves, and should not be used unsupervised for sleep unless your OT has specifically advised it.

If you tell Julia the child's age and weight, and what you want the product for, she will suggest a weight and size before anything is made.

Washing and care

  • Gentle cycle with a small amount of mild detergent.
  • No fabric softener.
  • Line dry where you can. Reshape while damp so the weight stays evenly spread.
  • Check the stitching every so often, the way you would with any product a child uses hard.
